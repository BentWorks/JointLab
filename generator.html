04 / EXPORT

Ready for a test print?

Export two watertight polygon-extruded solids. The STL files contain millimeter-valued coordinates; import at 1:1 scale into Bambu Studio.

  1. Import both STLs into Bambu Studio.
  2. Print the smaller test pair first, ideally with identical orientation to your full part.
  3. Test insertion force, fit and wear; revise clearance if necessary.
  4. Do not rely on this connection to resist lengthwise withdrawal without a separate retention design.
