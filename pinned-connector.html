A removable pin that blocks pull-out.

An FDM-friendly three-piece cross-pinned tongue and sleeve. Align the two square through-holes after sliding the tongue into its sleeve, then insert the separately printed pin. The pin bridges both parts to resist longitudinal separation.

Dimensions

The pin is removable and blocks lengthwise movement while installed. It is not captive: vibration or inversion could allow it to back out. The geometry is experimental and needs physical testing. Do not use in safety-critical applications.
